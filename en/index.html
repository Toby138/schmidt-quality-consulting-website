My approach

A good test strategy does not mean more tests. It means better control.

My approach turns quality into a management instrument, along three interlocking lines of effect.

Test to Know

Know where you stand.

Make risks, maturity and remaining uncertainties visible: with a clear test strategy, meaningful metrics and verifiable entry and exit criteria.

Release decisions based on facts instead of gut feel.

Test to Grow

Structures that hold.

Evolve test strategy, V&V governance and processes so that programs become more stable, more efficient and more predictable.

Programs that deliver reliably and predictably.

Test Together

Everyone on one goal.

Align development, test, quality and management on shared goals and criteria. Quality is not created in one department. It is created through collaboration.

No nasty surprises at the interfaces.

What this means for you Fewer late surprises, confident release decisions and programs that management and customers trust again.

A feedback loop, not a one-off project

The three lines of effect depend on each other: transparency shows where structures need to improve. Better structures deliver more reliable information. And neither holds unless all disciplines share the same goals and criteria.

This is how quality moves from a downstream inspection step to a management tool for the program: it provides orientation earlier, makes conflicting objectives visible before they escalate, and creates the basis for decisions that hold.
