Test Strategy & V&V Governance

When quality needs to be managed more effectively from the start.

The situation

Quality is not created in test.

Quality is not created in test. It is created through clear objectives, clean accountabilities, effective quality gates and solid decision bases across the entire development process.

I help companies build or evolve a test and V&V governance that makes quality visible earlier, risks manageable and release decisions more confident.

Value & outcomes

What you get out of it.

Typical outcomes

  • test strategy and V&V target picture
  • quality gates with clear decision criteria
  • release readiness model
  • entry, exit, suspend and resume criteria
  • roles and accountabilities
  • defect management
  • KPI and reporting concept
  • management transparency
Business impact

Higher test effectiveness, better control, fewer late surprises and more confidence in release decisions.
