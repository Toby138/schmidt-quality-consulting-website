Test Strategy Assessment

When it is unclear where the biggest quality and governance risks sit.

The situation

First see where the biggest levers are.

Many organizations test a great deal yet still see too late where the real risks, inefficiencies or governance gaps lie.

A test strategy assessment quickly creates a solid picture of how effective your current test and quality assurance is, and which levers deliver the greatest benefit.

Value & outcomes

What you get out of it.

Typical outcomes

  • analysis of the existing test and quality assurance
  • assessment of risks, maturity and decision-making capability
  • identification of the biggest levers for test effectiveness and efficiency
  • concrete recommendations for action
  • 90-day roadmap
  • a management-ready basis for decisions
Business impact

Earlier risk transparency, better prioritization, optimized cost of quality and a clear plan of action instead of activity for its own sake.
